One place for the firm's daily work

Built around how a CA office actually runs: departments, branches, monthly GST cycles and clients who send photos on WhatsApp.

WhatsApp and email intake

Clients send files the way they already do. Each file is recognised and placed under the right client, with nothing to sort by hand.

Client assignments

Every piece of work moves step by step, from documents received to filed. Nothing is missed, and everyone can see where it stands.

GST computations

GSTR-1, GSTR-3B (monthly and quarterly), PMT-06 and CMP-08, prepared from the client's documents and sent for partner approval.

Income-tax computation

Salaried returns worked out under both regimes from Form 16 and proofs, with every figure traceable to its source document.

Compliance calendar

Due dates for every client in one view, marked automatically as returns are filed.

Client updates

Clients hear from the firm at each step, on WhatsApp or email, in the firm's own name. Fewer "what's the status?" calls.
